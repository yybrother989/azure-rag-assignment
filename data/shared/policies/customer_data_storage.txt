CUSTOMER PAYMENT DATA STORAGE POLICY
Effective 2024-06-15. Owner: Security & Compliance. Compliance basis: PCI-DSS v4.0.

1. Scope
Applies to ALL customer payment-card data handled by company systems,
whether transmitted, processed, or stored. Includes web checkout flows,
mobile checkout flows, batch import from partner systems, and any
exported reports that touch payment data.

2. What we may store

   2.1 Tokenised PAN (Primary Account Number): YES, only the token issued
       by our PCI-compliant payment processor. Never the raw PAN.

   2.2 Last 4 digits of the PAN: YES, for receipts and customer service.

   2.3 Cardholder name: YES, encrypted at rest.

   2.4 Card expiry date: YES, encrypted at rest.

3. What we MUST NEVER store

   3.1 Raw (un-tokenised) PAN — under any circumstance.
   3.2 CVV / CVC / CID — the 3- or 4-digit security code. NEVER stored
       after the authorising transaction completes, even encrypted.
   3.3 Magnetic stripe track data, EMV chip cryptograms, PIN, or PIN block.
   3.4 Card data in plain-text logs, email, Slack, ticketing systems, or
       analytics events.

4. Encryption requirements
All data permitted under section 2 must be encrypted AT REST using
AES-256-GCM with keys held in the company HSM (Hardware Security Module).
Keys are rotated every 12 months. Decryption is only authorised within
the payment-service boundary; no other service may hold a decryption key.

5. Access controls
Access to the payment-service decryption keys is restricted to the
on-call SRE rotation and the Payments team lead. All access is logged
to the immutable audit store and reviewed weekly.

6. Data retention
Tokenised payment data is retained for 7 years for tax and dispute
purposes, then irreversibly deleted. See the broader Data Retention
Policy for non-payment data.

7. Incident response
Any suspected exposure of cardholder data is a P0 incident. Page the
on-call Security lead within 15 minutes of detection.
