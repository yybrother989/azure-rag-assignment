DATA RETENTION POLICY
Effective 2025-01-01. Owner: Security & Compliance.

1. Scope
This policy covers all logs, telemetry, and user activity records produced by
company-operated systems and devices (Device Alpha, Device Bravo, the web
console, and any internal service that emits structured log data).

2. Retention windows

   2.1 User activity logs (web console actions, API calls, login events):
       retained for 90 DAYS in hot storage, then permanently deleted.

   2.2 Device telemetry (sensor readings, health metrics):
       retained for 365 DAYS in cold storage. After 365 days the records are
       irreversibly deleted.

   2.3 Personally Identifiable Information (PII) embedded in any of the above:
       retained for AT MOST 30 DAYS, regardless of the surrounding record's
       retention class. PII includes names, email addresses, phone numbers,
       IP addresses tied to an identified user, and physical addresses.

   2.4 Security audit trails (authentication failures, privileged operations,
       configuration changes): retained for 2 YEARS to satisfy SOC 2 controls.

3. Deletion mechanism
Deletion is performed by a daily job (03:00 UTC) that purges records older
than the policy windows above. Deletion is irreversible: there is no
"trash" period and no recoverable backup of expired records.

4. Exceptions
The Security team may issue a Legal Hold to extend retention for specific
records during investigations. Legal holds are tracked in the GRC system and
override the retention windows above for the duration of the hold.

5. Review
This policy is reviewed annually by Security & Compliance and may be amended
to reflect regulatory changes.
