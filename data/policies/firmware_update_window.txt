FIRMWARE UPDATE MAINTENANCE WINDOW POLICY
Effective 2025-02-01. Owner: Field Operations.

1. Production fleet update window
All firmware updates to production-deployed Device Alpha and Device Bravo
units MUST be scheduled within the approved maintenance window:

   - Day:  Sunday
   - Time: 02:00 - 06:00 customer-local time
   - Frequency: at most one update per device per calendar week

Updates outside this window require a CAB (Change Advisory Board)
exception ticket with at least 48 hours' notice.

2. Staged rollout
Production firmware updates are rolled out in three rings:

   Ring 0 (Canary):     1% of fleet, 24 hours soak.
   Ring 1 (Early):      10% of fleet, 48 hours soak.
   Ring 2 (General):    remaining 89% of fleet.

A regression in any ring halts promotion to the next.

3. Rollback
Every update must ship with a tested rollback path. If a customer reports
a regression on the new firmware within 7 days of their device entering
Ring 2, Field Ops will roll that customer's device back at no charge,
within the same maintenance window.

4. Test devices
Internal test units and lab fixtures are NOT bound by this policy.
Engineers may push experimental firmware to lab devices at any time.

5. Notification
Customers on a paid support tier are notified 7 days in advance of each
production firmware push, with the changelog and the maintenance window
they will fall into based on their fleet ring assignment.
