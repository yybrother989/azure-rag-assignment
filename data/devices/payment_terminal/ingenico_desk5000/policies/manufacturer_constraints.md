# Ingenico Desk/5000 Manufacturer Constraints

## Manufacturer-derived rules
- Use only the recommended power supply for the terminal.
- Maintenance should be performed only by authorized technicians.
- Perform cable connection work while the terminal is powered off.
- Keep the original packaging for shipping and returns.
- Use manufacturer-approved thermal paper of the correct size.
- Disconnect power before maintenance operations.
- Use indoor placement on a stable surface and keep the device away from heat, dust, vibration, and unsuitable environments.

## Security-related manufacturer guidance
- Check regularly for signs of tampering.
- Restrict privileged access to trusted staff.
- Position the terminal to make PIN spying difficult.
- Never ask the customer to disclose a PIN.

## Not covered here
This file does not define:
- bank transaction policy
- internal fraud workflow
- PCI operational ownership
- terminal replacement approval process
