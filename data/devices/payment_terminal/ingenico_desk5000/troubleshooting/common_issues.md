# Ingenico Desk/5000 Common Issues

## 1. Terminal does not turn on
Check:
- power supply cable seating
- electrical outlet availability
- that the provided power supply is being used

## 2. Terminal does not connect to the telephone line
Check:
- telephone line cable
- line availability / dial tone
- line configuration / called number if applicable

## 3. Terminal cannot read cards
Check:
- magnetic stripe orientation and swipe speed
- condition of the magstripe
- correct chip insertion and removal timing

## 4. Ticket is not printed
Check:
- roll presence
- paper orientation
- thermal paper type
- proper lid closure

## 5. Cleaning / maintenance uncertainty
Before maintenance:
- disconnect power
- unplug cables
- use a soft cloth lightly dampened with soapy water
- do not use solvents, detergents, or abrasives
- do not clean electrical connections

## Escalation boundary
Escalate when:
- power and line checks are normal but the terminal remains unavailable
- tampering is suspected
- a secure component such as SAM / SIM handling is required outside normal local support
