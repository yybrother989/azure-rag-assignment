# Ingenico Desk/5000 Paper and Card Issues

## Paper roll issues
### Symptom
Receipt is not printed.

### Check
- paper roll is present
- roll is positioned correctly
- thermal paper is being used
- the sensitive side of the paper is correct
- the cover is fully closed

### Replacement reminder
A red line on the paper indicates the roll is nearing the end.

## Card reading issues
### Magnetic stripe card not read
Check:
- card orientation
- magstripe facing the terminal body as instructed
- swipe speed is steady, neither too slow nor too fast
- stripe is not damaged, cracked, or badly worn

### Chip card issues
Check:
- chip card is inserted in the correct direction
- card remains inserted until the transaction flow allows removal

### Contactless issues
Check:
- card is held close to the contactless active zone
- it remains there during the transaction
- no conductive label or obstruction is placed on the contactless area

## When to stop self-debug
Stop and escalate if:
- multiple known-good cards fail in the same way
- receipt printing still fails after paper checks
- there are visible signs of tamper or physical damage
