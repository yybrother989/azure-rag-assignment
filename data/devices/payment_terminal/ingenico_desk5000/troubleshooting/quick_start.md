# Ingenico Desk/5000 Quick Start

## Purpose
Use this guide for basic installation, first-line paper setup, and common transaction-side checks.

## Before you start
Confirm:
- the correct vendor power supply is available
- the terminal is placed on a flat indoor surface
- the required connection type is known (telephone, Ethernet, or optional interfaces)
- the terminal is powered off before cable work

## Package checklist
Typical box contents include:
- terminal
- power supply and cable
- telephone or Ethernet connection cable
- user guide
- paper roll already fitted with the terminal

## Basic setup
1. Open the down-side trapdoor.
2. Connect the required cables.
3. Connect power.
4. Close the trapdoor.
5. Plug the power supply into the mains.

## Connection notes
The rear connection area may include:
- telephone
- power
- Ethernet
- USB host / slave
- RS232
- optional SIM / SAM / MicroSD depending on model

## Paper roll loading
1. Open the paper compartment from the rear catch.
2. Insert the paper roll in the correct orientation.
3. Pull the paper upward.
4. Close the lid and press until it clips.

## Daily use reminders
- red key: cancel
- yellow key: clear / long press to feed paper
- green key: validate
- use the correct motion for swipe / chip / contactless reading

## Escalate when
Escalate instead of continuing self-service if:
- the terminal does not power on after power checks
- the terminal cannot establish the required line connection after basic checks
- card reading continues to fail with multiple known-good cards
- tamper / security concerns are suspected
