# Epson TM-m30II Manufacturer Constraints

## Manufacturer-derived rules
- Use the specified AC adapter.
- Do not overload power outlets.
- Do not disassemble or modify the product.
- Do not use aerosol sprays with flammable gas near the printer.
- Do not set up or handle cables during a thunderstorm.
- Do not connect cables in ways other than described in the manual.
- Place the unit on a firm, stable, horizontal surface.
- Unplug before extended periods of non-use.
- Disconnect power before maintenance and cleaning.
- Use care around the cutter and roll paper cover.

## Connectivity / configuration boundaries
- Use documented interface setup methods and utility tools.
- Do not assume simultaneous wired LAN and Wi-Fi use.
- Bluetooth security should be configured appropriately when Bluetooth is enabled.
- Optional accessories and interface modes should follow the documented setup process.

## Not covered here
This file does not define:
- store printer deployment policy
- receipt retention rules
