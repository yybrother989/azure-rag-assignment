# Canon CR-120 Manufacturer Constraints

## Manufacturer-derived boundaries
- Install Scanning Utility before connecting the scanner.
- Use the provided power cord and approved cable practices.
- Use the provided USB cable or a Hi-Speed USB 2.0 compatible cable.
- Disconnect power before cleaning or maintenance.
- Do not disassemble or modify the scanner.
- Do not block the air vents.
- Keep liquids, metal objects, and volatile chemicals away.
- Do not place the scanner on unstable or vibrating surfaces.
- Do not open the maintenance cover while operating.
- Do not turn the scanner upside down.

## Handling boundaries
- Avoid unsuitable media and mixed-thickness batches that may cause feeding errors.
- Remove clips, staples, and similar fasteners before scanning.
- Use care around moving parts and paper edges.

This file does not define:
