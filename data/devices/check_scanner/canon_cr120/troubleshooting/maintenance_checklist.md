# Canon CR-120 Maintenance Checklist

## Daily / routine checks
- power off before cleaning
- disconnect power before maintenance
- inspect feed path and visible debris
- clean scanning glass and rollers as needed
- keep the area around the scanner stable, clean, and well ventilated

## Cleaning areas called out in the manual
- main unit exterior
- feed path
- scanning glass and rollers
- magnetic head
- ink jet nozzles
- cleaning cards (where applicable)
- rollers and disposal tank as replacement items

## Environment and handling reminders
- avoid unstable surfaces
- avoid blocking air vents
- keep liquids and metal objects away
- avoid humid or dusty locations
- do not open the maintenance cover while the scanner is operating
- do not turn the scanner upside down because ink may leak from the disposal tank

## Operator reminders
- remove clips, staples, and fasteners before scanning
- avoid unsuitable paper types
- take care during jam clearing because paper edges may cut fingers
- store or move the scanner only after power-off and cable removal

## Escalate when
Escalate when:
- rollers appear worn and require replacement
- repeated jams continue after cleaning and gap checks
- power behavior is abnormal
- there is physical damage, smoke, odor, or unusual heat
